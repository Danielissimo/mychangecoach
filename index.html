About Daniele dell'Erba

Delivering change and empowering transformation since 1998.

Daniele dell'Erba is an international coach, change management consultant, author, and trainer. Starting his career in the London office of Accenture, he established his practice to help leaders and international organisations navigate complex behavioural, cultural, and strategic shifts.

He holds a Master's degree in Organisational Psychology from the London School of Economics (LSE) and was awarded the European Union Marie Curie Fellowship for Doctoral research in Change Management across Rome, Lisbon, and London. Daniele teaches in Master's and Executive Education programs at UCP – Católica Lisbon School of Business & Economics and at ISEG – Lisbon School of Economics & Management. He works with a wide variety of corporate clients and industries across Europe and beyond.

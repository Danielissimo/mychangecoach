About Daniele dell'Erba

Delivering change and empowering transformation since 1998.

Daniele dell'Erba is an international coach, change management consultant, author, and trainer. Starting his career in the London office of Accenture, he established his practice to help leaders and international organizations navigate complex behavioral, cultural, and strategic shifts.

He holds a Master's degree in Organizational Psychology from the London School of Economics (LSE) and was awarded the European Union Marie Curie Fellowship for Doctoral research in Change Management across Rome, Lisbon, and London. He works with a wide variety of corporate clients and industries across Europe and beyond.
